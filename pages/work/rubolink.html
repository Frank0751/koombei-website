Finding a trustworthy plumber should not take three phone calls.

RuboLink connects Ghanaian homes and businesses with vetted local professionals across plumbing, electrical work, cleaning, and AC repair. It is built and operated by KoomBei Digital Limited, which makes it the project we are hardest on.

The problem

Hiring a tradesperson in Ghana usually runs on word of mouth. You ask a neighbour, who gives you a number, which turns out to be someone's cousin who may or may not answer. If they do show up, price is negotiated on the spot with no reference point, and there is no recourse if the work is poor.

The problem cuts both ways. Skilled, honest professionals have no way to prove they are skilled and honest to someone who has never met them. Their reputation stops at the edge of their own neighbourhood.

  • No reliable way for a customer to verify a worker before letting them into their home
  • No price transparency, so every job starts with an uncomfortable negotiation
  • Good workers stuck inside a referral network they cannot grow beyond
  • Payment handled in cash, with no record for either side
2

What we built

RuboLink is a two-sided marketplace, which means the hard part was never the interface. It was trust. Every design decision came back to one question: would someone feel safe letting this person into their house?

The platform is built in React and deployed on Vercel, with Mobile Money and card payments so a customer can pay the way they already pay for everything else in Ghana.

  • Background-checked professionals, with verification status visible before booking
  • Service categories covering plumbing, electrical, cleaning, AC repair, and more
  • Mobile Money and card payment, so cash is never the only option
  • Coverage across six-plus cities, with location-aware matching
  • A booking flow built mobile-first, because that is how the entire audience browses
  • Worker profiles that let a good reputation travel beyond one neighbourhood
