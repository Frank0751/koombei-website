Payment terms.

Agreed in writing before anything starts, so both sides know where they stand.

To start
50 percent of the agreed price, or the full amount if you prefer to settle once. Work begins when it clears and the brief is signed off.
Before handover
The remaining 50 percent when the finished site is approved. It runs on our staging address until then, and goes live on your domain with every login handed over the day the balance clears.
The delivery clock
Starts once the deposit is received and all your content, text, images and logos, has been submitted.
Domain renewal
Billed separately after year one, at prevailing rates.
How to pay
Bank transfer or MTN Mobile Money in Ghana. International clients agree the most convenient option, usually bank transfer, confirmed in the proposal.
Who owns it
You do. On final payment the site, its code and every login are yours.
