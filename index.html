Everything you want to know before we start building together.

Three tiers — Seed (GHS 2,000, up to 5 pages, 7–14 days), Rise (GHS 3,000, up to 8 pages, 14–21 days, 1 month free maintenance), and Summit (GHS 6,000, up to 15 pages, 21–30 days, 3 months free maintenance). All packages include design, development, hosting setup, and SSL.
Our packages start at GHS 2,000 for the Seed package. The Rise package is GHS 3,000 and the Summit package is GHS 6,000. Individual services are also available standalone. NGOs and nonprofits receive up to 20% off any package.
Delivery timelines depend on the package — 7–14 days for Seed, 14–21 days for Rise, and 21–30 days for Summit. We also send a full proposal within 48 hours of your discovery call. All timelines start from the date we receive your content and full brief.
Yes. Verified NGOs and nonprofits receive up to 20% off any package. We believe organisations doing important mission-driven work deserve the most support. Mention your NGO status in the discovery form and we will confirm your discount in the proposal.
It takes three steps: fill out our discovery form on the Contact page (about 5 minutes), we review your brief and send a tailored proposal within 48 hours, then we agree on scope and begin building. Head to the Contact page to kick things off.
Yes — KoomBei works with clients worldwide. The entire process is remote-friendly: a discovery form, video calls, and live previews at every stage. Your website is deployed on a global edge network, so it loads fast wherever your audience is, and we schedule around your time zone.
Our base prices are set in Ghana Cedis (GHS). The prices you see on this site are automatically converted into your local currency and US dollars as a live approximation. Your proposal confirms the exact amount and the currency we agree on before any work begins.
In Ghana we accept Mobile Money (MoMo and AirtelTigo) and bank transfer. For international clients, we agree on the most convenient option — typically bank transfer — and confirm it in your proposal. We work with a deposit before starting and the balance on delivery, and every payment detail is confirmed in writing before any work begins.
Hosting setup is included in every package. We deploy on Vercel, connect your custom domain, configure DNS and SSL, and manage the setup for 12 months. You do not need technical knowledge — we handle all of that, and we are available if anything ever needs adjusting.
After launch you receive post-launch support — 2 weeks on Seed, 1 month on Rise, 3 months on Summit — covering bug fixes and minor changes. After that, our maintenance plans start from GHS 100 per update. We walk with you long after launch day.
